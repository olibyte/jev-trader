# Rubric

The judge scored each candidate from 1 to 5 on each criterion, with one sentence of evidence.

1. **Honesty.** The chance that a shown learner win is luck, per verdict and across the site, including nightly re-looks and small block counts. Resistance to gaming with many rules, many accounts, or clones.
2. **Reachability.** The measured chance that a rule with a real edge (d = 0.5 or 1.0) shows a win within 1 to 5 live years, and whether that chance depends on strangers' rules.
3. **Cost.** Compute per night, storage, build effort, the need for accounts, and whether a save changes any other verdict.
4. **Fit with settled decisions.** Three answers, a simultaneous 95% interval decides, verdicts from the live period only, and every rule logged before any result.
5. **Explainability.** Whether one paragraph explains the verdict to a curious learner.
6. **Evidence quality.** Whether the numbers are reproducible, labelled, and consistent across candidates.
