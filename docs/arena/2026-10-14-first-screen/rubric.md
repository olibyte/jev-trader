# Rubric (picker only, candidates do not see it)

Score each 1 to 5.

1. **Obvious action.** A non-technical visitor knows what to do within 5 seconds and completes the main action in at most 2 taps.
2. **Repeatable takeaway.** The visitor leaves with one sentence they could tell a friend, and that sentence is true to the milestone 7 data (no overclaiming a winner; the "could be luck" idea lands without statistics words).
3. **Plain language with depth on demand.** Zero banned jargon on the first screen. A technical visitor still finds something worth it, on screen or one tap away.
4. **Product, not experiment.** Reads like something a person would use or share, not a research report. Not about Jev.
5. **Small to build.** Fits `web/` with the existing `export/arena/v1/` data and no new research or backend.
6. **Works on a phone.** Readable at 390 px with no horizontal scroll, and the main action fits in the first screenful.
